The PSC pipeline is installed.

It runs on the schedule "{{ .Values.schedule }}" and writes its results to PostgreSQL.

To start a run now:

  kubectl create job --from=cronjob/{{ include "psc-pipeline.fullname" . }} {{ include "psc-pipeline.fullname" . }}-manual

To connect to the database:

  kubectl port-forward svc/postgres 5432:5432
  psql "postgresql://{{ .Values.postgres.auth.username }}@localhost:5432/{{ .Values.postgres.auth.database }}"
